# Final Dissertation Claims–Evidence Matrix

This matrix controls important factual, methodological, and empirical claims proposed for the final dissertation, ensuring that each is traceable to exact evidence before use.

| Claim ID | Dissertation Section | Proposed Claim | Repository Evidence | Result/Evidence Artifact | Literature Citation | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Allowed status values: `VERIFIED`, `PENDING`, and `REJECTED`.

1. `VERIFIED` requires exact evidence.
2. `PENDING` must not be stated as a final empirical finding.
3. `REJECTED` claims must not appear in final dissertation prose.
4. Repository evidence supports experiment, methodology, and results claims but is not an academic literature citation.
5. Literature claims must use approved references.
6. Interim v2.6 values remain `PENDING` until final verified analysis.
7. Do not infer evidence from absence.
8. Every major numerical result should eventually identify its exact result artifact, version/run where relevant, and generating analysis step.
